Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

My Writing App ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a web-based writing application available at https://my-arts.github.io/my-writing-app/ .

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how users can control their data.

1. Google Account Sign-In

The App uses Google OAuth to allow users to sign in with their Google Account. We do not receive or store your Google Account password.

When you sign in, Google provides the App with the account information and authorization necessary for the features you approve.

2. Google Drive Data

The App is designed to store your writing data in your own Google Drive. This may include documents, journal entries, chapters, sections, application settings, and version history created through the App.

Your writing data remains in your Google Drive. The App accesses Google Drive only to provide the writing, synchronization, backup, and version-history features that you authorize.

The App does not sell your Google Drive data or use your writing content for advertising.

3. Version History

The App may store previous versions of your writing so that you can view, compare, and restore earlier versions.

Restoring an earlier version does not intentionally erase the newer version. The restored state is saved as a new version so that version history can be preserved.

4. Local Storage and Offline Data

The App is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It may store application data locally on your device using browser storage technologies such as IndexedDB and service-worker caches.

Local storage is used to provide offline functionality, improve performance, and retain changes that have not yet synchronized with Google Drive.

5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share your writing content with advertisers or other third parties.

Your data may be processed by Google services when necessary to provide Google Account authentication and Google Drive functionality. Google's own terms and privacy policies apply to Google's services.

The App does not intentionally transmit your writing content to an independent application server for advertising, analytics, or AI training.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not intentionally use advertising trackers or third-party analytics services to analyze the contents of your writing.

7. Security

The App uses HTTPS when accessed through GitHub Pages and uses Google's OAuth authorization mechanism for Google Account access.

No application can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their Google Account and devices from unauthorized access.

8. Revoking Access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google Account through your Google Account security settings.

Revoking authorization may prevent the App from accessing or synchronizing your Google Drive data.

9. Your Data

Because writing data is stored in your Google Drive, you retain control over that data through your Google Account and Google Drive.

You can manage, download, move, or delete files created by the App through your Google Drive.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the minimum age required to use a Google Account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact the application developer through the support contact provided on the App's Google OAuth consent screen.
